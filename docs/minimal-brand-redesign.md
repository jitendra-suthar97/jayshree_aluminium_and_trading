# JAYSHREE website redesign — minimal brand direction

## Design intent
Position JAYSHREE Aluminium & Trading as a considered local aluminium-and-glass fabrication brand. The experience should feel calm, precise, architectural and trustworthy rather than like a generic template or a crowded product catalogue.

## Visual system
- **Palette:** warm white surfaces, soft stone section backgrounds, charcoal typography, restrained brushed-bronze accents.
- **Typography:** retain the locally hosted Archivo display face and Inter body face; use larger editorial headlines, tighter display tracking and readable body copy.
- **Layout:** generous whitespace, thin dividers, simple square-edged geometry and restrained hover motion.
- **Logo:** keep the owner-supplied PNG unchanged and on a light surface. Do not recolour, trace, crop or regenerate it.
- **Imagery:** use only real photographs of completed aluminium and glass work. Never present AI-generated images or stock imagery as completed customer projects.

## Homepage hierarchy
1. **Header:** quiet sticky navigation, smaller logo treatment, simple active state and one clear “Start a project” action.
2. **Hero:** short brand statement, concise service description, project/contact actions and proof points. Real hero photography appears only when an owner-supplied image is configured.
3. **Trust strip:** four factual proof points: aluminium and glass, made-to-measure, fabrication-to-fitting, and Sanchore/Jalore location.
4. **Services:** uncluttered service list with clear titles, short descriptions and restrained text-first cards.
5. **Projects:** keep the portfolio section hidden until at least three genuine featured project photos are available.
6. **Process:** explain enquiry, site measurement, fabrication, glass fitting/hardware and installation.
7. **Enquiry:** keep phone, WhatsApp and quote form routes accessible without making every section feel like an advertisement.
8. **Footer:** clear contact details, services, company navigation and location.

## Content and trust rules
- Do not publish dealer/authorised wording until the exact wording and permission are verified.
- Do not add invented years of experience, project counts, testimonials, awards, warranties, turnaround promises or service areas.
- Confirm phone/WhatsApp routing and opening hours with the owner before treating them as final.
- Keep the product scope to aluminium and glass. Do not introduce wood, marble, granite or unrelated materials.

## Asset checklist before launch
- [ ] One strong real installation photo for the homepage hero (wide desktop crop and portrait mobile crop).
- [ ] At least three real completed project photos to enable the portfolio section.
- [ ] Three to six real Instagram/work photos if the homepage Instagram strip is desired.
- [ ] Owner-approved About introduction and confirmed service-area list.
- [ ] Confirmed opening hours, WhatsApp number, map pin and any dealer wording.
- [ ] A mark-only favicon derived from an owner-approved source asset (not an AI recreation).

## Implementation notes
This first pass updates the shared design tokens, homepage hero, header, trust strip and service cards. Existing routes, SEO metadata, contact components, accessibility patterns and mobile sticky contact actions are retained. Review the preview on mobile and desktop, then add genuine photography and refine interior pages to match the same visual system.
